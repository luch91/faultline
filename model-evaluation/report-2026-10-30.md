# Minsk controlled live comparison, 2026-10-30

## Method and scope

The eight held-out scenario definitions and reference outcomes were committed in `55682f0` before the comparative calls. Each case supplied a validated workflow with two conditions, one policy rule, and one execution step. Four cases contain an unenforced mandatory condition, two contain an enforced mandatory condition, one has unknown required evidence, and one tests an optional condition. The expected outcomes were reasoned from those declared policy and gate relationships and cross-checked offline against the deterministic evaluator before scoring. The cases are synthetic and structurally similar, so these numbers do not estimate real-world accuracy.

Forty primary model and case tasks and eight repeated tasks were completed. The primary tasks included eight Spark extractions, eight Spark tool-assisted investigations, eight Wonder tool-assisted investigations, eight Baseten challenger proposals, and eight direct Spark analyses without simulator tools. One unsafe and one unknown case were repeated for the investigation methods. The direct baseline did not call the simulator; its selected intervention and prediction were checked afterward against the reference evaluator. Baseten supplied candidate interventions only; it did not perform extraction or safeguard proposal. The assisted routes used the same deterministic `run_counterfactual` and `propose_safeguard` tools. Failed responses remained in the denominator.

The complete-task score also requires selecting the locked intervention target. On H08 both Meta assisted models and the direct baseline selected the mandatory test condition instead of the optional signature condition. Their chosen change produced a real `SAFE_BLOCK`, but it did not answer the locked H08 task, whose reference outcome is `SAFE_EXECUTION`. This is a target-selection mismatch, not an unsafe-execution false positive. On the H07 repeat, direct Spark predicted `UNSAFE_EXECUTION` even though the independent check returned `INDETERMINATE`, producing one false positive on the single repeated unknown case. Spark and Wonder assisted investigations each produced a second verified repair on the H01 repeat. Baseten returned no candidate content on the H01 repeat.

The initial extraction scorer checked schema, the main policy condition, gate relationship, and exact source spans. A stricter post-run scorer also checked the observed value. It found that H07's text said review evidence was not available, but the extracted observation was false instead of unknown. Thus exact source quotation did not ensure correct interpretation. All eight extractions had valid source spans; seven of eight preserved the observation and passed the strict combined check. No labels or prompts were changed after observing this failure.

## Usage and cost

The protected local Worker used one persistent Durable Object ledger for Meta and Baseten. The entire run, including the initial Spark pilot, made **60 provider dispatches** against an **80-dispatch configured limit** and the owner's **$10 combined conservative reservation ceiling**. The ledger's admission calculation retained the larger of each call's initial reservation and token-priced usage, totaling **$1.386602**. This leaves **$8.613398** of the authorized conservative ceiling unused. The provider-reported token counts priced with the documented rates total **$0.330163**; that figure excludes failed calls without usage. Neither provider returned a final billed currency charge, so actual provider expenditure and promotional-credit deductions are unconfirmed. The reservation is a local dispatch guard, not a provider-side billing cap.

## Failures and interpretation

- Baseten's challenger produced no valid intervention in H04 and H06 and no candidate content in the H01 repeat. These are counted as failures, not abstentions.
- Spark extraction converted unavailable review evidence into false on H07. An extracted workflow with a valid schema and matching source span can still be semantically wrong.
- Spark and Wonder assisted paths both verified four of four available unsafe repairs in the primary set. The direct baseline proposed no independently verified safeguard, so its correct unsafe classification does not establish repair ability.
- Wonder matched Spark on the eight primary tasks and cost more in token-priced usage. This sample shows no measured benefit from routing these cases to Wonder.
- These inputs were generated from one small template with varied requirement terms. They are useful integration and failure-mode checks, not a statistically strong held-out corpus or production deployment study.

## Reproduction and evidence

`pnpm exec tsx scripts/score-live-comparison.ts` regenerates the scored file from the committed [raw evidence](evidence-2026-10-30.json). The [scored results](scored-2026-10-30.json) contain all 48 task rows, per-model metrics, the ledger snapshot, and a SHA-256 digest of the raw evidence. The [locked labels](ground-truth.json) and [case generator](heldout.ts) establish the inputs and expected outcomes. Running the live harness again requires the private local Worker and a new budgeted evaluation session; it is not part of the offline reproduction command.

The public Minsk preview still has inference disabled. No PR was merged and no production deployment was made.
